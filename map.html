THE COURAGE TO LOVE · A MINDFUL MOMENT

Your Map to
Mindful Connection

Before you react, find your way back to connection.
A 2-minute mindset reset for moms raising teenage daughters.
1 / PAUSE
Notice
What am I feeling?
Am I hurt, worried, frustrated, or rejected?

Name the emotion without judging yourself.
2 / REFLECT
Question
What story am I telling myself?
Am I assuming she doesn't respect, love, or need me?

An assumption isn't necessarily the truth.
3 / RECONSIDER
Reframe
What else could be true?
Could she be overwhelmed, needing space, or having a hard day?

Consider another possibility.
4 / INTEND
Choose
How do I want to show up?
What response reflects the relationship I want to build?

Choose your intention before your words.
“My daughter's behavior doesn't define my worth as a mom. I can pause, question my assumptions, and choose how I respond.”
Small efforts. Real connection. ♡ Blessings, Kristin
